# SpendLessBro 

A simple student budget tracker. Track your money, see where it goes, stress less.

No sign up. No server. Just open and use.

---

## What it does

- Track your monthly income and expenses
- See a chart of your spending by category
- Get simple tips based on your spending habits
- Export your data to Excel/CSV
- Reset at the start of each month

---


## Files

```
spendlessbro/
```
